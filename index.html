Anything else? Ask us

Do you charge extra after hours or on weekends?

No. The call-out fee is the same seven days a week, and work is quoted as a fixed price whatever the time.

Are your technicians licensed?

Yes. Plumbers, gasfitters, electricians and refrigerant-handling technicians each hold the licence for their trade. Licence numbers are listed at the top and bottom of every page.

Can one booking cover more than one trade?

Yes. Tell us about the whole job and we'll schedule the right people together, for example an electrician and an air conditioning installer for a new split system.

How do I know what it will cost?

You pay the call-out fee. The technician then diagnoses the problem and gives you a written fixed price. Nothing starts until you approve it.

What if something goes wrong after the job?

Our workmanship is guaranteed for life. Call us and we'll come back and fix it at no charge. Manufacturer warranties apply to parts.

While you wait

Make it safe in the next few minutes.

Burst pipe or major leak

  1. Turn off the water at the mains tap, usually near the meter at the front of the property.
  2. If water is near power points or lights, switch off that circuit at the switchboard, but only with dry hands and feet.
  3. Move valuables and open a tap to drain the lines.

Sparking, burning smell or electric shock

  1. Don't touch the fitting, the appliance or anyone in contact with it.
  2. If it's safe to reach, turn off the main switch at the switchboard.
  3. If there's fire or someone is hurt, call 000 first.

You can smell gas

  1. Don't use light switches, phones or flames inside.
  2. Turn off the gas at the meter if you can do it safely, and open doors and windows.
  3. Leave the building, then call your gas distributor's emergency line, or 000 if there's immediate danger.

Air conditioning failed in extreme heat

  1. Check the isolator switch beside the outdoor unit and the circuit at the switchboard. Reset once only.
  2. Close blinds, run fans and move to the coolest room.
  3. Tell us if there's an elderly person, a baby or a medical need in the home and we'll prioritise the visit.
Danger to life? Call 000 for fire, injury or a serious gas leak before you call us.
